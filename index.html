01 / ABOUT

Research-driven engineering.
Practical robotic systems.

My work sits at the intersection of intelligent control, human–robot interaction, and simulation-led robotics development.

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Mechatronics, focusing on shared-control autonomous vehicles using Brain–Machine Interface signals and Model Predictive Control. Alongside this research, I work on industrial robot digital twins, motion-control integration, and ROS-based simulation.

I enjoy taking ideas from mathematical models and algorithms into working simulations and hardware-connected prototypes — with careful attention to what is measured, modelled, and still needs validation.

  • Experience with Delta DVP50MC motion control, EtherCAT and CANopen servo drives.
